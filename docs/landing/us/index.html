The problem

Some sounds you just can’t miss.

The doorbell, the smoke alarm, a car horn, the baby waking up. If you can’t hear them, you depend on someone being there to tell you. And if you’re family, you live with the question: what if it goes off and they don’t notice?

Someone’s at the door The smoke alarm goes off The baby is crying A car honks

How it alerts you

Something sounds. You feel it.

  1. Something important happens

    A knock, the doorbell, a smoke alarm, a car horn or the baby crying.

  2. FortWatch recognizes it

    In seconds it knows what it was, among 10 important everyday sounds.

  3. It vibrates and shows you what happened

    You feel it on your wrist and read it at a glance: big letters and a color for each alert.

Who it’s for

More independence for you. More peace of mind for your family.

For you

Go on with your day.

If someone knocks or an alarm goes off, you feel it on your wrist and read it at a glance. No waiting for someone to tell you.

For your family

Worry less.

If your mom or dad doesn’t hear well, FortWatch tells them when the doorbell rings or the smoke alarm goes off. They keep living their life.
