# User research

## Sierra, Sierra College

Sierra is deaf and studies at Sierra College in Nevada City, California. Nico met her
before Cal Hacks 13.0.

- She can't afford an Apple Watch, the usual option for sound alerts on the wrist.
- When she goes around the city, she waits until someone can go with her.

> It feel such a lack of independence every time I want to go out in the city alone.
> I wish I had an Apple watch, but you know, it's out of budget. I wished I had a
> cheaper alternative specifically for sensing important noises.

Photo: [sierra-with-nico.jpg](sierra-with-nico.jpg). Sierra is on the left, Nico in the
middle.

**Before anything goes public (Devpost, pitch slides, a public repo), get Sierra's OK
to use her name, quote and photo.**

## What this means for SoundSense

- **Community:** deaf and hard-of-hearing community college students. Price is the
  barrier, so the pitch compares our parts cost with an Apple Watch.
- **Where she needs it:** outside, in the city. Today the listener is a Mac within
  Bluetooth range, which works at home or in class but not on a street. Outdoors needs
  the phone as the listener (iPhone app on the roadmap) or the on-wrist microphone.
- **Sounds that matter outside:** car horns, sirens, someone shouting. These are
  already Safety alerts.
