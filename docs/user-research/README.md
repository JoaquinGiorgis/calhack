# User research

## Sofia, Sierra College

Sofia is deaf and studies at Sierra College's Nevada County campus in Grass Valley, California, a community college. Nico met her before Cal Hacks 13.0. She agreed to us using her name,
quote and photo.

- She can't afford an Apple Watch, the usual option for sound alerts on the wrist.
- When she goes out in the city, she waits until someone can go with her.

> I feel such a lack of independence every time I want to go out in the city alone.
> I wish I had an Apple watch, but you know, it's out of budget. I wished I had a
> cheaper alternative specifically for sensing important noises.

Photo: [sofia-with-nico.jpg](sofia-with-nico.jpg). Nico is on the left, Sofia on the
right.

## What this means for FortWatch

- **Community:** deaf and hard-of-hearing community college students. Price is the
  barrier, so the pitch compares our parts cost with an Apple Watch, which also needs
  an iPhone.
- **Where she needs it:** outside, in the city. That needs the microphone on the
  wristband and sound recognition running on the board itself, with no Mac nearby.
- **Sounds that matter outside:** car horns, sirens, someone shouting. These are
  already Safety alerts.
